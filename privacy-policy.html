Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Effective: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hindu Calendar 2027: Panchang mobile application ("Calendar 2027", the "App", "we", "us"; package com.shubhcalendar.hindupanchang) handles information, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By installing or using the App you agree to this Policy.

The App has no account or login. All Panchang timings — tithi, nakshatra, sunrise, Rahu Kaal, muhurat and festival dates — are calculated on your device. Your events, reminders, city and settings are stored only on your device; we do not run servers that receive them.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Settings — language, colour theme, city, calendar preferences and notification choices, stored on your device.
  • My Events — birthdays, anniversaries, notes and reminders you add. They are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Location (optional) — if you tap "Use my current location", the App reads your device's approximate location once to calculate local sunrise and Panchang timings. The coordinates are stored only on your device. To show a city name, the App asks Android's built-in Geocoder, which may contact your device's location provider (for example Google) under that provider's own privacy policy. You can pick a city from the list instead and never grant location permission.
  • Purchase records — if purchases are offered, they are handled by Google Play Billing. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Support messages — anything you send to innovatroid@gmail.com, including the app version and device details the App pre-fills in feedback emails.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • App installation ID — a random identifier from Firebase Installations, used to load remote app configuration and to enforce abuse blocks.
  • Usage & device data — app opens, screens viewed, session length, device model, Android version, app version, language and approximate region (derived from IP address), collected by Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used and improve it.
  • Advertising data — the Android Advertising ID and ad interaction data (ads shown or clicked), collected by Google AdMob to serve, measure and frequency-cap ads.
  • Crash reports — if the App crashes, a report with the stack trace, device model, Android version, app version and installation ID is sent to Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. It does not include your events or location.

1.3 Permissions we request

  • Approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, optional) — only for "Use my current location".
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — daily Panchang, festival and vrat reminders, and your event reminders. You can turn these off in Settings.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — to reschedule your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Internet & Network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — to load remote configuration, show ads and send analytics and crash reports. Panchang calculations work fully offline.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — used by AdMob and Analytics. You can reset or delete it in your device's Google settings > Ads.
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) — lets Google Play process purchases if they are offered.

2. How We Use Information

  • To calculate and show Panchang, festivals and muhurat for your city.
  • To send the reminders and notifications you turn on.
  • To understand which features are used and improve the App.
  • To find and fix crashes and other bugs.
  • To display ads — banner, interstitial, app-open and optional rewarded ads — and measure their performance.
  • To process purchases and confirm entitlement, where offered.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, and to comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

3. Third-Party Services

The following services receive data when you use the App. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Google Firebase (Realtime Database for app configuration, Installations, Google Analytics for Firebase, Crashlytics crash reporting) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play Billing & In-App Review — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Android Geocoder / location services — only when you use "Use my current location", under your device provider's privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising

The free App is supported by ads from Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are optional and unlock a feature in exchange for watching. Where required by law, you will be shown a consent choice for personalised or non-personalised ads, which you can change later in your device's Google settings > Ads.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Events, reminders, location and settings stay on your device until you delete them in the App, clear its data or uninstall it. If you have enabled Android backup, your own Google backup may include them.
  • Installation ID is reset when you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for Firebase for up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Data held by Google services (AdMob, Firebase, Play) is kept according to Google's retention policies.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example India under the DPDP Act 2023, the EEA, UK, or California under the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Email innovatroid@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Your events and settings are kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Network connections to Google services use TLS. No system is perfectly secure; contact us if you have a concern.

8. Children

The App is a general-audience calendar and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version, and significant changes will be highlighted in the App.
